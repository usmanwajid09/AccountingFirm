QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Bookkeeping FAQs

How do we share our financial data with you?

We establish shared access folders using Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive, and connect directly to your digital bank feeds via secure open banking APIs in Xero or QuickBooks. Receipts can be snapped and uploaded using Hubdoc or Dext.

Which accounting systems do you support?

We are fully certified across all major cloud systems, including Xero, QuickBooks Online, Sage Business Cloud, and FreeAgent. If you run a custom legacy database, we can audit and import your CSV ledgers manually.

How often do you balance and reconcile our accounts?

By default, we run reconciliations weekly to ensure your bank feeds match ledger entries. For high-volume clients (like e-commerce brands), we run daily updates to ensure credit flows are tracked with absolute accuracy.

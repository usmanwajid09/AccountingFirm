QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Planning FAQs

How do you establish our budget targets?

We analyze 12 to 24 months of historic ledger data, reconcile recurring overhead commitments, and interview your lead partners on expansion plans. We combine this with sector benchmarks to set realistic, achievable budget targets.

What does cash runway modeling entail?

For cash-burning startups or fast-growing operations, we calculate the Net Burn Rate (monthly overhead minus net collections). We divide this by cash reserves to identify exactly how many months of solvency remain, plotting alerts for when safety targets are crossed.

How often do we review budget variances?

Monthly. We compare actual reconciled bank ledger items against the approved budget model, identifying cost overruns or revenue shortfalls immediately to allow for rapid operational course-corrections.

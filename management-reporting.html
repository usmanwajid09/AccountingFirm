QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Reporting FAQs

What is included in a monthly management report package?

Each monthly pack includes: Executive Summary commentary, Profit & Loss statement (showing target comparisons), Balance Sheet audit, Cash Flow statement (indirect method), Accounts Receivable/Payable aging metrics, and visual KPI charts.

Can you prepare custom reports for my industry?

Yes. We customize reports to track the metric metrics that matter to your business model, such as Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and LTV for tech firms, or occupancy margins and labor cost metrics for hospitality operations.

What is the monthly turnaround time for report delivery?

We typically deliver reporting packages within 7 to 10 business days following the month-end closing, depending on the speed of bank feed reconciliation and transaction uploads.
